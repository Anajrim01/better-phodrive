How this works

The share is read from the part of the link after #. That part stays in this browser. Google Photos is read through this site's proxy, and the file is rebuilt and hashed here.

Limits

Free tier: about 100,000 proxy requests per day, four byte streams at once, and an 8 MiB cap on range requests. Large files download one chunk at a time.
